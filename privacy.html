Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Information We Process

Depending on how you use the app, we process account information such as your name, if provided through Sign in with Apple, your email address, account identifier, and the phone number provided during onboarding, which we use to connect your account to your app data; profile information such as age, height, weight, goals, dietary preferences, limitations, and medical history; and information you enter about meals, workouts, sleep, and coach chats. If you enable notifications, we also process information needed to deliver them, such as a push notification token and time zone.

How We Use Information

We use this information to operate your account and app features, provide personalized coaching, send reminders you enable, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information.

AI Coaching and Service Providers

AI coaching is optional. If you enable it and provide the required consent, relevant information you enter may be sent to OpenAI to generate coaching responses. OpenAI says API data is not used to train its models by default. OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring logs containing prompts and responses for up to 30 days; longer retention may apply when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect its services or another party from harm.

We use service providers to operate the app, including Supabase for authentication, Railway for hosting and database services, and Expo and Apple for push notification delivery. These providers process information as needed to provide their services.

Your Choices and Account Deletion

You can turn AI features on or off from the AI DATA option in Settings. You can disable push notifications through your device settings. You can request account deletion from the app’s Settings. When deletion succeeds, we delete your account and its associated profile, health logs, weight history, workout records, weekly reports, coach conversations, and coach memories from our application database, and delete your authentication account. Information held by service providers, including in backups or other records, may be retained according to their policies or applicable legal requirements.
